Good to Know

Sizing, Shipping & Returns

Every product page includes a printable foot-measurement guide. If you're between sizes, our stylists recommend sizing up and adding an insole for a snugger fit — just mention it when you order.

We recommend ordering 8–10 weeks before your wedding date to allow time for made-to-order sizing and any exchanges. Rush production is available for an added fee — just ask.

Unworn pairs can be exchanged for a different size or style within 30 days of delivery, free of charge. We suggest trying them on a carpeted surface at home before your wedding day.

Yes — we ship worldwide from our Colombo studio. International orders typically arrive within 7–14 business days, and tracking is provided at checkout. Shipping cost is calculated at checkout based on destination.
