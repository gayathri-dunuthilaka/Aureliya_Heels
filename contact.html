Before You Order

How to Measure Your Foot Size

Follow these simple steps at home, then send us your measurements in the order form below.

  1. 1

    Prepare a flat surface

    Place a plain sheet of A4 paper on a hard, flat floor.

  2. 2

    Stand naturally

    Step onto the paper with your full body weight on that foot.

  3. 3

    Trace your foot

    Hold the pen upright and carefully trace around your entire foot.

  4. 4

    Repeat for the other foot

    Trace both feet because they can differ slightly in size.

  5. 5

    Measure length and width

    Measure from heel to longest toe and across the widest point.

  6. 6

    Enter your size in the form

    Use your measurement to fill in the shoe size field with your order.

Tip: Trace your foot while barefoot or wearing thin socks for a clearer outline.
