Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

We recommend ordering 10–30 days before your wedding date to allow time for made-to-order sizing and any exchanges. Rush production is available for an added fee — contact us and we'll do our best to help.

Several styles are available in ivory, blush, and dyeable satin. Send us a swatch of your gown fabric and our studio will confirm the closest match before production begins.

Unworn pairs can be exchanged for a different size or style within 30 days of delivery, free of charge. We suggest trying them on a carpeted surface at home before your wedding day.
